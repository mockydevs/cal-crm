# apps/dashboard

The dashboard: a static React app built by Vite.

`pnpm dev` at the root runs it with the server. Vite passes `/api` on to the server, at the
`PORT` in the root `.env` (3000 by default).

```bash
pnpm --filter @repo/dashboard dev            # http://localhost:5173
pnpm --filter @repo/dashboard build          # then checks the size budgets
pnpm --filter @repo/dashboard test:e2e       # Playwright and axe, against the build
pnpm --filter @repo/dashboard i18n:extract   # after changing any text
```

## Layout

```
src/routes/   one file per page; TanStack Router turns them into routes and chunks
src/shell/    the sidebar and page frame around every page, and the sign-in pages' frame
src/locales/  translation catalogs (PO files), extracted from the code
e2e/          end-to-end tests, at 1440 px and 360 px
```

## Signing in

Every page of the app sits under the pathless `_app` route
(`src/routes/_app.*.tsx`). Its guard asks who's signed in and sends anyone signed out to
`/sign-in?next=…`, which brings them back afterwards, by password or by an emailed link.
`/forgot-password` and `/reset-password` reset a password. `src/auth.ts` calls Better
Auth's endpoints at `/api/auth`.
Only paths on this site are followed after signing in.

People who use an authenticator app enter its code, or a recovery code, at
`/sign-in/two-factor`, and the sign-in page signs in with a passkey too. When someone's agency
requires two-factor sign-in, the guard sends them to `/set-up-two-factor` first.
**Settings > Sign-in and security** sets it up, adds passkeys, and lets agency staff
require it of the team (`src/security/`).

This suite stands in for those endpoints (`e2e/session.ts`, `e2e/auth.spec.ts`); `@repo/e2e`
signs in for real, through Mailpit.

## Businesses

A business's pages are under `/b/{its slug}/…`, and the agency's under `/agency/…`. The guard also asks
`GET /api/v1/me` where the person may work (`src/workspace.ts`):
- **`/`** opens their only business. Otherwise it opens the agency's list of businesses for
  its staff, or a list to choose from for anyone else.
- **`/b/$slug`** finds the business among those they may enter. Pages read it with
  `useBusiness()`. Any other slug shows the not-found page, which doesn't say whether the
  business exists.
- **`/agency`** is for agency staff only; anyone else goes back to `/`. It lists the
  agency's businesses, and **New business** adds one (name, time zone and currency, from the
  browser's own lists), then opens it.

**The switcher** at the top of the sidebar moves between businesses and keeps the section
the page is in (`src/shell/business-switcher.tsx`).

Each request names the business in the page's address with the `Business-Id` header
(`apiIn(business.id)` in `src/api.ts`). Each query's key starts with `['b', business.id]`.
So a tab shows and changes only the business in its own address, whatever other tabs do.
Signing in clears the query cache, so nothing cached for someone signed in before carries
over.

`src/routeTree.gen.ts` is generated by TanStack Router when the app builds or runs; it's
committed, and neither edited nor linted.

## Text

Every word people read is marked for translation: `<Trans>` in JSX, `` t`…` `` from
`useLingui()` in code, and `` msg`…` `` for text defined outside a component. A lint rule
rejects unmarked text, and `check` fails while the catalogs lag behind the code.

## Budgets

`scripts/check-budget.mjs` runs after every build. It fails when the first load is over
200 KB of gzipped JavaScript, or a page's own chunk is over 100 KB.
